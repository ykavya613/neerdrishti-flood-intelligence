# Nirmal District Flood Intelligence Profile

## 1. District Overview & Hydrography
* **District**: Nirmal
* **Terrain & Elevation**: Hilly escarpment of the Sahyadri range with steep forest catchments.
* **Primary River Basin**: Godavari River main channel and Kadam River (Kadam Narayan Reddy Reservoir).

## 2. Major Vulnerability Factors
* **Flash Flood Inflow from Catchment**: Steep gradient forest streams channel massive sudden inflows into Kadam Reservoir during deep depressions over Maharashtra/Telangana borders.
* **Kadam Project Inflow Thresholds**: Historical events (2022/2023) saw inflows exceeding 3.5 lakh cusecs against safe spillway design, requiring rapid downstream evacuation.
* **Low-Lying Habitats**: Khanapur, Dasturabad, Sofinagar, and GMR colony in Nirmal town.

## 3. Critical Flood Response Guidance
* **Flash Flood Warnings**: Immediate alert when Kadam dam reservoir water level approaches 700 ft with inflow >1.5 lakh cusecs.
* **Downstream Safety**: Pre-emptive evacuation of 18 low-lying riparian villages along the Kadam and Godavari floodplains.
