# NeerDrishti System Overview & District Directory

## 1. Scope & Jurisdiction
NeerDrishti covers all 33 administrative districts of the State of Telangana, India:
- **Priority Flood Monitoring (5)**: Hyderabad, Warangal, Nirmal, Mancherial, Bhadradri Kothagudem.
- **Standard Monitoring (15)**: Adilabad, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Jogulamba Gadwal, Kamareddy, Karimnagar, Khammam, Kumuram Bheem Asifabad, Mahabubnagar, Medak, Mulugu, Nalgonda, Nizamabad, Peddapalli, Rajanna Sircilla, Rangareddy.
- **Limited Data Districts (13)**: Hanumakonda, Jagtial, Jangaon, Mahabubabad, Medchal Malkajgiri, Nagar Kurnool, Narayanpet, Sangareddy, Siddipet, Suryapet, Vikarabad, Wanaparthy, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri.

## 2. Multi-Signal Fusion Architecture
The platform fuses five primary signal vectors:
1. Rainfall Telemetry (Automatic Weather Stations & IMD Grid)
2. River Gauging & Dam Telemetry (CWC & Irrigation Dept)
3. Topographical Elevation & Slope Models
4. Crowdsourced Citizen Incident Reports
5. Urban Stormwater Drain & Nala Inundation Records

## 3. Bilingual Support
NeerDrishti supports full conversational intelligence in English and Telugu (తెలుగు).
