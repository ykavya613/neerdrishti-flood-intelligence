# Hyderabad District Flood Intelligence Profile

## 1. District Overview & Hydrography
* **District**: Hyderabad (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation - GHMC area)
* **Terrain & Elevation**: Deccan Plateau undulating topography ranging from 490m to 610m above MSL.
* **Primary River Basin**: Musi River (tributary of Krishna), Esa River, Hussainsagar Lake surplus catchment.

## 2. Major Vulnerability Factors
* **Rapid Urbanization & Impervious Surfaces**: Over 65% paved/built-up land cover prevents natural rainwater infiltration.
* **Nala Encroachments**: Historical chain of cascading lakes (Cheruvus) has been fragmented; several primary stormwater drains (nalas) face severe cross-sectional constriction.
* **Low-Lying Micro-Basins**: Concentrated flash flood risk in Begumpet, Alwal, Nadeem Colony (Tolichowki), Ramanthapur, and Moosarambagh causeway.

## 3. Critical Flood Response Guidance
* **Flash Flood Threshold**: Rainfall exceeding 40 mm/hr creates rapid street inundation within 30 minutes.
* **Actionable Evacuation Protocols**: Evacuate residents along Musi riverbed settlements (Chaderghat, Moosarambagh) when Osmansagar and Himayatsagar gates are opened.
* **Citizen Guidance**: Avoid underpasses (malakpet, khairatabad), stay tuned to GHMC Disaster Response Force (DRF) updates.
