# Mancherial District Flood Intelligence Profile

## 1. District Overview & Hydrography
* **District**: Mancherial
* **Terrain & Elevation**: Riverine floodplains and coal mining plateau adjacent to the Godavari basin.
* **Primary River Basin**: Confluence point of Pranhita River (major tributary) with the Godavari River at Kaleshwaram/Mancherial border.

## 2. Major Vulnerability Factors
* **Pranhita-Godavari Backwater Effect**: Heavy discharges from Maharashtra via Pranhita back up into local streams (Rallavagu, Bokkalavagu), submerging riverine colonies.
* **Mining Area Runoff**: Altered natural drainage due to Singareni open-cast mining overburden dumps leading to accelerated silt runoff into local waterways.
* **Low-Lying Urban Zones**: Godavarikhani bypass, Mancherial railway underbridge, Luxettipet, Chennur floodplains.

## 3. Critical Flood Response Guidance
* **Backwater Monitoring**: Real-time water level tracking at Pranhita confluence gauge stations.
* **Key Precautions**: Clear heavy machinery and miners from low-lying open-cast pit zones when rainfall >80 mm is recorded within 12 hours.
