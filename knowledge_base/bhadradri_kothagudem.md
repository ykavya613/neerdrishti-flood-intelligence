# Bhadradri Kothagudem District Flood Intelligence Profile

## 1. District Overview & Hydrography
* **District**: Bhadradri Kothagudem (including temple town Bhadrachalam)
* **Terrain & Elevation**: Downstream Godavari gorge valley with dense deciduous forest cover and extensive riverine floodplains.
* **Primary River Basin**: Lower Godavari River main stem, Sabari River, Kinnerasani River.

## 2. Major Vulnerability Factors
* **Upstream Dam Discharges**: The entire upstream Godavari catchment (Medigadda, Sripada Yellampalli, Polavaram backwaters) discharges through the narrow Bhadrachalam river valley.
* **Critical Flood Stages at Bhadrachalam**:
  - *1st Warning*: 43.0 Feet
  - *2nd Warning*: 48.0 Feet
  - *3rd Warning (Danger)*: 53.0 Feet (In July 2022, water crested at historic 71.3 ft).
* **Isolation of Tribal Mandals**: Dummugudem, Cherla, Allapalli, and Burgampahad lose road connectivity as causeways submerge rapidly.

## 3. Critical Flood Response Guidance
* **River Stage Protocols**: Initiate mandatory evacuation of Vista Complex, Subhashnagar, and Kotha Colony when river stage crosses 48 ft.
* **Emergency Supplies**: Pre-position NDRF boats, diesel generators, and drinking water tankers in Cherla and Dummugudem before 3rd warning is declared.
