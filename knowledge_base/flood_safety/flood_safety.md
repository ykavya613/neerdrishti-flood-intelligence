# Flood Safety & Public Protection Guidelines (NeerDrishti)

## 1. Immediate Life Safety Principles
* **Turn Around, Don't Drown**: Never walk, swim, or drive through moving floodwaters. Just 15 cm (6 inches) of moving water can knock down an adult, and 30 cm (12 inches) can sweep away a light motor vehicle.
* **Avoid Low-Lying Underpasses & Causeways**: Urban underpasses and rural bridge causeways can fill with water within minutes during intense downpours.
* **Stay Clear of Drainage Canals & Nalas**: Open stormwater drains (nalas), riverbanks, and lake surplus channels experience rapid surge velocities and high suction hazards during flash floods.

## 2. Electrical & Structural Safety
* **Avoid Electrical Infrastructure**: Do not touch submerged electrical poles, transformers, low-hanging cables, or waterlogged junction boxes. Report sparking or exposed wires immediately to TSSPDCL / TSNPDCL.
* **Structural Integrity**: Stay away from dilapidated walls, weakened structures, and culverts that show signs of soil scouring or erosion.

## 3. Household Preparedness & Evacuation
* **Emergency Kit**: Keep an emergency supply kit ready containing potable drinking water, dry food rations, essential medicines, first-aid items, a torch/flashlight with extra batteries, and a charged mobile power bank.
* **Important Documents**: Store identity documents, insurance policies, and critical records in waterproof bags or elevated locations.
* **Follow Official Directives**: Heed all alerts, advisories, and instructions issued by the Telangana State Disaster Management Authority (TGSDMA), District Collectors, and Municipal Disaster Response Forces (DRF/NDRF).

## 4. Post-Flood Safety & Hygiene
* **Water Purification**: Boil all drinking water or use certified chlorine purification tablets to prevent water-borne illnesses like cholera and gastroenteritis.
* **Drain Inspection**: Do not attempt to clear blocked deep culverts or manholes manually without trained municipal rescue personnel.
