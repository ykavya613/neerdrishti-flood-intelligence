# Telangana River Basins & Hydrological Routing (NeerDrishti)

## 1. Major River Basins in Telangana
1. **Godavari Basin**:
   * **Upstream Channels**: Originates in Western Ghats, enters Telangana at Kandakurthi (Nizamabad / Nirmal).
   * **Tributaries**: Kadam, Pranhita, Penganga, Manair, Indravati, Sabari, Kinnerasani.
   * **Key Monitoring Nodes**: Kadam Dam (Nirmal), Sripada Yellampalli (Peddapalli/Mancherial), Medigadda/Kaleshwaram (Jayashankar Bhupalpally), Bhadrachalam (Bhadradri Kothagudem).
   * **Downstream Propagation**: Discharges from Nizamabad/Nirmal/Adilabad take 3–6 hours to reach Mancherial/Peddapalli, 6–12 hours to Jayashankar Bhupalpally/Mulugu, and 12–24 hours to Bhadradri Kothagudem.

2. **Krishna Basin**:
   * **Upstream Channels**: Enters Telangana near Jogulamba Gadwal / Narayanpet from Karnataka.
   * **Tributaries**: Bhima, Tungabhadra, Musi, Dindi, Munneru, Paleru.
   * **Key Monitoring Nodes**: Jurala Project (Jogulamba Gadwal), Srisailam Dam (Nagar Kurnool), Nagarjuna Sagar (Nalgonda).
   * **Downstream Propagation**: Outflows from Jurala propagate toward Nagar Kurnool and Nalgonda in 6–18 hours.

3. **Musi Sub-Basin (Urban Catchment)**:
   * **Origin & Course**: Originates in Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad), flows through Hyderabad and Rangareddy into Nalgonda/Suryapet to join the Krishna River.
   * **Critical Chokepoints**: Osmansagar, Himayatsagar, Moosarambagh Causeway, Chaderghat, Nagole Bridge.
   * **Downstream Propagation**: Discharges from Vikarabad reach Hyderabad within 2–4 hours and flow down to Yadadri Bhuvanagiri and Nalgonda within 6–12 hours.

4. **Manair Sub-Basin**:
   * **Origin & Course**: Originates in Rajanna Sircilla, flows through Karimnagar into Peddapalli to join the Godavari.
   * **Key Reservoirs**: Upper Manair, Mid Manair (Rajanna Sircilla), Lower Manair Dam (Karimnagar).

5. **Munneru Sub-Basin**:
   * **Origin & Course**: Originates in Mahabubabad, flows through Warangal and Khammam into Andhra Pradesh.
   * **Downstream Propagation**: Runoff from Warangal / Mahabubabad reaches Khammam town within 4–8 hours during heavy depressions.

6. **Tungabhadra Sub-Basin**:
   * **Course**: Borders Jogulamba Gadwal before confluence with the Krishna River near Alampur.
