# Warangal District Flood Intelligence Profile

## 1. District Overview & Hydrography
* **District**: Warangal (Warangal Urban / Hanamkonda & Rural)
* **Terrain & Elevation**: Low-lying granitic basin at ~270m MSL surrounded by rocky hillocks.
* **Primary River Basin**: Lower Godavari sub-basin, connected through Kakatiya-era historic tanks (Bhadrakali Tank, Waddepally Lake, Dharmasagar).

## 2. Major Vulnerability Factors
* **Interconnected Tank Breaches & Overflow**: Overflow from upstream tanks breaches downstream cascading channels.
* **Naimnagar Nala Bottlenecks**: Heavy siltation and construction on the Naimnagar Nala causes severe backflow inundating Hunter Road, Subedari, and Kakatiya University lowlands.
* **Saturated Black Cotton Soil**: Prolonged monsoon spells quickly reach soil saturation, causing immediate surface runoff.

## 3. Critical Flood Response Guidance
* **Critical Alerts**: Trigger alert whenever Bhadrakali reservoir reaches full reservoir level (FRL 1150 ft) during active downpours.
* **Key Shelter Zones**: Higher ground centers in Hanamkonda collectorate ridge and University sports complex.
